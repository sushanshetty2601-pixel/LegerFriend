Handling AI & Bookkeeping Ambiguity

The 3 Types of Suspense Accounts

Instead of making inaccurate assumptions, LedgerFriend segregates questionable items into 3 recognized accounting suspense categories:

Type 1 Suspense (Unknown Source / Missing Bill): Money entered or left your bank/cash, but you don't know who sent it or what bill it corresponds to. Resolve once the customer or invoice is identified.
Type 2 Suspense (Trial Balance Balancing Difference): In manual books or initial transfers, if debit totals don't equal credit totals, the difference is parked here until discovered by your Chartered Accountant.
Type 3 Suspense (Cheque Clearing & Dishonour Float): Cheques deposited that haven't cleared, or disputed bank debits/overdrafts under investigation.
